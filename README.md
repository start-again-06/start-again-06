## About Me

Anjan Mahapatra is a tech-savvy, analytical builder with a strong engineering foundation and a growing expertise across AI/ML, product management, strategy, growth, and financial decision-making. He has worked across manufacturing, construction, and SaaS, contributing to end-to-end product journeys spanning problem discovery, market research, product strategy, system architecture, user workflows, data-driven analysis, growth planning, financial evaluation, and go-to-market execution.

He brings together technical depth, product thinking, business strategy, and financial acumen to build solutions that are not only innovative, but also scalable, commercially viable, and outcome-driven.

With a multidisciplinary engineering and computational background, Anjan focuses on emerging AI paradigms including LLMs, SLMs, AGI, Physical AI, and Agentic AI systems. He is particularly interested in designing intelligent and increasingly autonomous systems that bridge digital intelligence with real-world applications.

His approach combines technical architecture with product vision, strategic thinking, usability, growth orientation, and financial awareness—enabling him to evaluate opportunities not only from a technology perspective, but also through the lens of customers, markets, economics, and long-term business impact.
